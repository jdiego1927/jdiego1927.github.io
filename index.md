## Who I am
I'm a Physics Engineer graduated with honors from Universidad del Cauca
(Colombia), now based in **Canada** and fully focused on the intersection of
data science, machine learning, and biomedical applications.

My engineering background gives me something most data scientists don't have:
a deep intuition for the physical world behind the numbers. I don't just fit
models, I understand why the data behaves the way it does.

## What drives me
I'm driven by complex problems and the challenge of turning uncertainty into understanding. Curiosity has always been the engine behind my career.

From physics and engineering to machine learning and data analytics, I've continuously sought opportunities to learn new tools, explore new industries, and solve unfamiliar problems.

I believe the best professionals are lifelong learners, and I'm committed to constantly expanding my skills through hands-on projects and real-world applications. For me, data is more than numbers — it's a tool for making better decisions, discovering opportunities, and solving real-world challenges.

## Beyond the data
Moving to Canada was a defining step in my life, one that required rebuilding both personally and professionally.

Outside of work and study, I’m drawn to activities that reflect the same curiosity that drives my professional life: sports, photography, drawing, and exploring new places. These experiences train me to observe carefully, think visually, and stay comfortable navigating unfamiliar environments.

I see all of these elements as part of the same mindset: curiosity, discipline, and the desire to understand the world from different perspectives.
