## Published research — ECG signal analysis for heart rate variability
*Göttingen research online publications · February 28, 2024*

A methodological proposal for computing heart rate variability (HRV) from
electrocardiographic signals, specifically designed for surgical patients and
a high-complexity scenario where signal quality is critical.

**Highlights:**
- ECG signal processing pipeline with tachogram computation and HRV feature analysis
- Strong correlation (0.99) between pre-processed signals and reference
- Low mean absolute error (0.0069 mV)
- AUROC of 0.96 in distinguishing normal from artifact-laden tachograms

**Tech:** ECG signal processing · HRV analysis · Python · Statistical methods
